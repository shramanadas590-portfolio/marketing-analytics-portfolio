# Ecommerce Sales and Customer Analysis

**Academic portfolio project**

## Business question

What do historical sales, product categories and customer patterns suggest investigating next?

## Tools

Power BI and Excel

## Data and scope

Historical academic sales data, January 2003–May 2005.

## Findings and work completed

The report reconciles approximately $9.60 million in revenue and describes 98 customers across 21 countries. Classic Cars and Vintage Cars contribute 58.8% of revenue.

## Recommendations and interpretation

Investigate November sales peaks, category margins and stock availability, and retention opportunities among high-paying customers. Revenue and customer payments are different measures and must retain their original labels.
