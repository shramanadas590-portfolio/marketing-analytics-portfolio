# iHotelier B2B Marketing Strategy

**Independent student case study; not affiliated with Amadeus**

## Business question

How could a 90-day campaign generate qualified demo requests from independent hotels in France?

## Tools

Market research, competitor benchmarking, Excel, Power BI and Canva

## Data and scope

Public-source product research and entirely simulated campaign performance. Research sources and access dates are recorded in the workbook.

## Findings and work completed

Compared iHotelier with SiteMinder and D-EDGE, developed audience hypotheses and proposed a 90-day campaign. The simulated scenario totals €3,500 spend, 58 new leads, 26 qualified leads and 9 booked demos.

## Recommendations and interpretation

The project connects audience selection, campaign planning, lead qualification and KPI reporting. Proposed actions include testing messages, following up with leads and monitoring cost per qualified lead.
