# Product Launch and Trade Marketing

**Independent portfolio simulations and proposals**

## Business question

How can launch planning and retail activation be structured and evaluated?

## Tools

Excel, presentation design, launch tracking and sales analysis

## Data and scope

Fictional prestige skincare launch and a proposed Dove retail test. All performance figures are simulated.

## Findings and work completed

Skincare: planned a launch across France, Germany and Spain, with retail and online channels, readiness tracking and a performance dashboard. Simulated sales are €78,500 versus a €78,000 target, with 68.3% sell-through. Dove: proposed a four-week in-store activation; the illustrative weekly average increases from 100 to 125 units.

## Recommendations and interpretation

Investigate Germany online performance in the skincare scenario. For a real Dove test, compare with a control store and check pricing, stock and costs before scaling.
