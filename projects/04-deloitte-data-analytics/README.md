# Deloitte Australia Data Analytics Job Simulation

**Forage job simulation; not employment at Deloitte**

## Business question

Which factory and device type show the most potential downtime?

## Tools

Tableau and Excel

## Data and scope

Daikibo telemetry and pay-equity exercise data supplied through Forage.

## Findings and work completed

The dashboard shows potential downtime of 480 minutes for Seiko (Osaka), compared with 420 for Shenzhen, 110 for Meiyo (Tokyo) and 20 for Berlin. With Seiko selected, LaserWelder accounts for all 480 minutes.

## Recommendations and interpretation

The interactive factory-to-device view helps prioritise further investigation. Values represent potential downtime under the exercise calculation, rather than independently measured production loss. The separate Excel exercise classifies equality scores.
