# GA4 Marketing Performance: September 2026

**Independent practice project**

## Business question

How do channels, landing pages and devices differ in reported marketing performance?

## Tools

GA4 and Excel

## Data and scope

Google Merchandise Store demo account, 1–30 September 2026. Separate from the historical ordered-session funnel project.

## Findings and work completed

Reported total revenue is $340,182.18. Direct accounts for $184,539.69. Checkout completion is 52.3% on desktop and 23.0% on mobile.

## Recommendations and interpretation

Review mobile checkout and event tracking. Investigate landing-page performance within comparable channel and device segments. Without advertising costs, ROAS and campaign profitability cannot be calculated.

## Limitations and reproducibility

The workbook explicitly documents a discrepancy: channel sessions sum to 140,051, while the GA4 reported total is 137,067, a difference of 2,984 with an unconfirmed cause. Do not silently reconcile these figures or combine these rates with the historical project.
